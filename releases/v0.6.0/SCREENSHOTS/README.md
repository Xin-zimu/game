# V0.6.0 Visual Evidence

`resource-distribution.png` is generated by `tools/render_resource_map.gd` from the same `TerrainGenerator.generate_chunk` output consumed by streaming renderers.

- Image: 576×576 pixels.
- Coverage: 6×6 chunks, or 192×192 world tiles, at three pixels per world tile.
- Origin chunk: `(-3, -5)`.
- Gold guide lines: exact 32×32 chunk borders.
- Dark background: generated biome colors.
- Resource markers: tree dark green, rock blue-gray, grass bright green, flower pink and berry bush medium green.
- Counts: tree 464, rock 225, grass 542, flower 265 and berry bush 194.

The image was visually inspected for empty water, biome-aware density, cross-border continuity and absence of severe overlap. Pairwise minimum spacing and water exclusion are also asserted against full-resolution coordinates in the automated suite.
