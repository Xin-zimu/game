## Review conclusion

- Every noise field has its own derived seed domain.
- Classification reads only seed, global integer coordinates and validated configuration.
- Biome IDs and byte codes are stable, unique and export-safe.
- Climate boundaries use configured transition targets before deterministic global-neighbour cleanup.
- No generator reads adjacent chunk objects, generation order or the player.
- Chunk checksums include every new map, so incompatible data cannot masquerade as generation format 2.
- Worker/main-thread ownership and bounded streaming behavior remain unchanged.
- Renderer modes and HUD are presentation-only and cannot alter generated bytes.
- Resources, harvesting and persistent chunk differences remain outside this release.

No release-blocking issue remains.
